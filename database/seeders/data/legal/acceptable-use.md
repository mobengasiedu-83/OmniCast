## Interpretation and scope

This policy applies to the OmniCast multi-tenant messaging platform offering SMS, outbound voice and email campaigns, associated templates, contact management, analytics, payment and wallet services. It supplements the OmniCast Terms and Conditions. Where mandatory law prevails, that law controls.

## 1. Authorised use

Use OmniCast only for lawful, transparent, properly authorised communications. Each client is responsible for recipients, content, sender identities, consent records, segmentation, campaign timing and legal compliance in each destination country.

## 2. Consent and permission

Upload or message only contacts for whom the client has an appropriate legal basis. Keep auditable evidence of consent where required, including source, date, notice and scope. Purchased, scraped, harvested or unlawfully shared lists are prohibited. An existing business relationship does not automatically permit every marketing communication.

## 3. Prohibited content

No phishing, impersonation, scams, malware, deceptive offers, illegal financial schemes, unlawful gambling promotions, threats, harassment, hate or discriminatory abuse, exploitation, or content violating third-party rights. No fraudulent collection of passwords, OTPs or payment credentials. Regulated or age-restricted promotions require prior written approval and applicable lawful permissions.

## 4. SMS-specific rules

Use only approved sender IDs and valid routing. Do not spoof originators, evade network filters, use deceptive shortened links or split campaigns to circumvent controls. Respect SMS encoding, segment charges, throughput limits, local quiet hours and operator rules. Promotional messages must include a functional opt-out method where required.

## 5. Voice-specific rules

Outbound voice campaigns must follow applicable consent, identification and time-of-day rules. Do not use deceptive caller IDs, unlawful robocalling, nuisance calling, repeated harassment or unauthorised call recording. Interactive DTMF and recorded audio must be disclosed and handled appropriately. Calls may be charged per answered destination using whole-minute rounding where the applicable published rate plan provides for it.

## 6. Email-specific rules

Authenticate sending domains as required (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), maintain accurate sender and reply-to details, include required sender contact information and unsubscribe facilities for marketing email, promptly honour opt-outs, and monitor bounces and complaints. No list bombing, open relays, misleading subject lines or unsolicited bulk email.

## 7. Templates, AI and personalisation

System templates are examples, not legal clearance. Clients must review accuracy, claims, placeholders and sensitive data. AI-generated copy or audio must not impersonate individuals without authority or mislead recipients. Clients remain responsible for final campaign content.

## 8. Campaign review and approvals

Campaigns may be screened automatically or manually and may be rejected, paused or returned for revision. Approval does not certify legality, guarantee delivery or transfer responsibility from the client. Maintain the existing approval workflow; approval must precede dispatch, including scheduled campaigns.

## 9. Suppression and complaints

Maintain opt-out, do-not-contact, bounce and complaint suppression records. Never re-import suppressed recipients to bypass restrictions. Respond promptly to abuse reports and provide consent evidence when reasonably requested.

## 10. Platform protection and fair use

We may apply rate limits, concurrency controls, per-account quotas, traffic shaping, sender verification, wallet reservations, delivery windows and anti-fraud checks. Attempting to bypass controls, probe other tenants, attack systems or interfere with shared infrastructure is prohibited.

## 11. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may warn, require remediation, reject or pause a campaign, disable sender IDs, suspend services, preserve evidence, notify providers or authorities when required, or terminate an account. Emergency restrictions may occur without prior notice where necessary to protect recipients or infrastructure.

## 12. Reporting and appeals

Report abuse to [ABUSE EMAIL] with campaign ID and supporting details. Clients may request review of enforcement decisions through [SUPPORT CHANNEL]. We may require consent evidence, content revisions or security remediation before reinstatement.

## 13. Responsibility and indemnity

Clients are responsible for unlawful recipient acquisition, content, targeting and campaign instructions, subject to the liability allocation and applicable law in the master Terms. This policy does not override mandatory consumer or data protection rights.
