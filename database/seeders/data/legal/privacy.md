## Interpretation and scope

This policy applies to the OmniCast multi-tenant messaging platform offering SMS, outbound voice and email campaigns, associated templates, contact management, analytics, payment and wallet services. It supplements the OmniCast Terms and Conditions. Where mandatory law prevails, that law controls.

## 1. Who we are and our roles

[LEGAL ENTITY] operates OmniCast. For account, payment, platform security and direct commercial administration data, the operator generally determines processing purposes. For customer-uploaded recipient lists and campaign content, business clients generally determine purposes and act as controllers, while OmniCast acts as processor under the applicable DPA. Roles depend on the actual activity and law.

## 2. Personal data we collect

Account and organisation details (name, email, phone, role, organisation and verification records); contacts and imported lists (names, numbers, email addresses, segmentation fields and consent evidence); message content, audio, attachments and templates; delivery metadata, call outcomes, opt-out events and campaign analytics; payment references, transaction status, wallet ledger and invoice records; IP address, device, login and audit logs; support tickets and communications. Payment card credentials should be handled by the payment provider, not stored by OmniCast.

## 3. Sources and purposes

Information comes from users, clients uploading recipient records, connected gateways and payment providers, and platform activity. Purposes include account provision, campaign delivery, recipient suppression, authentication, security, fraud prevention, billing, support, analytics, legal compliance and service improvement. We do not use client recipient lists for our own marketing without a separate lawful basis and authorization.

## 4. Legal bases and consent

Processing must have a lawful basis under applicable Ghanaian data protection law, including consent where required, contractual necessity, legal obligations and other bases permitted by law. Clients are responsible for establishing lawful grounds to contact recipients and for retaining evidence of consent or another applicable basis. Marketing consent and opt-out obligations vary by channel, recipient and jurisdiction.

## 5. Communications and recipient rights

Clients must maintain accurate contact data, respect preferences and suppression lists, provide required sender identity and opt-out mechanisms, and honour withdrawals. OmniCast may apply global or tenant-level suppression where legally necessary. Recipients should contact the sending organisation first about a campaign; we will support requests as required by law and contractual duties.

## 6. Sharing and international transfers

We may share necessary data with contracted hosting, telecommunications carriers, SMS aggregators, voice infrastructure, email delivery services, payment processors, security vendors and professional advisers. Subprocessors must be contractually bound to appropriate safeguards. Cross-border transfers require applicable legal safeguards and client instructions; specific locations and vendors should be disclosed in a maintained subprocessor register.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Account, financial and audit records are retained for documented business, legal and regulatory periods. Campaign recipient data, recordings and delivery events follow configurable retention schedules set out in the service agreement or DPA. On termination, clients may export eligible data during the agreed retrieval period, after which it is deleted or de-identified unless retention is legally required. Backups expire on documented cycles.

## 8. Security

Controls should include tenant isolation, role-based access, multifactor authentication for privileged users, encryption in transit, encryption at rest where appropriate, secret management, restricted access to recordings and exports, monitoring, vulnerability remediation, backups, incident response and audit trails. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Individual rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection and withdraw consent where relevant. We verify requests, distinguish controller and processor responsibilities, and forward recipient requests to the appropriate client where required. We will not disclose another client’s data.

## 10. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies support authentication and session security. Optional analytics or marketing technologies require appropriate notices and consent where applicable. A separate cookie notice should describe specific technologies, providers and retention periods.

## 11. Children and sensitive data

The service is intended for business use. Clients must not upload children’s data, health information, financial account credentials or other sensitive data unless specifically authorised, necessary and protected under appropriate lawful and contractual safeguards. Campaigns must not expose sensitive details in insecure SMS or email.

## 12. Automated decisions and profiling

Segmentation, delivery optimisation, fraud signals and automated throttling may be used. Clients remain responsible for their own targeting decisions. Where legally required, affected individuals will receive information and available safeguards concerning consequential automated decisions.

## 13. Breach notification

We will investigate suspected personal-data breaches, contain incidents, preserve evidence and notify affected clients without undue delay under the DPA. Regulator and individual notifications will be made by the responsible party within applicable statutory requirements.

## 14. Ghana regulatory framework

This policy is intended to align with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and relevant electronic communications and cybersecurity requirements, as applicable. Registration, notification and other obligations must be confirmed with Ghanaian counsel and relevant authorities before publication.

Material changes will be notified by appropriate in-product or email notice and will take effect on the stated date, subject to applicable law. Historical versions and acceptance records will be retained where appropriate.
