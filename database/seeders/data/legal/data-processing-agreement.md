## Interpretation and scope

This policy applies to the OmniCast multi-tenant messaging platform offering SMS, outbound voice and email campaigns, associated templates, contact management, analytics, payment and wallet services. It supplements the OmniCast Terms and Conditions. Where mandatory law prevails, that law controls.

## 1. Parties and incorporation

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is between [CLIENT LEGAL NAME] (Controller) and [OMNICAST LEGAL ENTITY] (Processor) and forms part of the OmniCast Services Agreement. The effective date, client details and signature blocks must be completed.

## 2. Roles and instructions

The Controller determines purposes and lawful grounds for recipient communications. The Processor processes client personal data only on documented instructions, including configured campaigns and service settings, unless law requires otherwise. The Processor will notify the Controller if an instruction appears unlawful unless prohibited.

## 3. Subject matter and duration

Processing covers contact import, segmentation, campaign preparation, SMS/voice/email delivery, delivery reporting, opt-outs, support and deletion throughout the services term and any agreed post-termination transition period.

## 4. Data subjects and categories

Data subjects may include customers, prospects, employees, parents, patients or other contacts selected by the Controller. Data may include names, telephone numbers, email addresses, organisation fields, personalised campaign variables, consent metadata, delivery/call events, recordings and message content. Sensitive data is excluded unless expressly agreed with additional safeguards.

## 5. Controller obligations

Controller shall establish lawful bases, provide privacy notices, manage consents and opt-outs, ensure accuracy, minimise sensitive data, configure retention and respond to data-subject requests. Controller must ensure content and contact lists are lawful in each destination.

## 6. Processor personnel and confidentiality

Processor limits access to authorised personnel subject to confidentiality obligations and appropriate training. Access is logged and reviewed; privileged permissions are restricted and revoked promptly when no longer needed.

## 7. Technical and organisational measures

Maintain tenant isolation, MFA for administrators, least privilege, encryption in transit, secure key/secret management, protected backups, patching, vulnerability assessment, audit logs, secure development and change control, incident response, business continuity and deletion controls. Specific controls and exceptions must be recorded in Schedule B.

## 8. Subprocessors

Processor may engage approved hosting, telecom, messaging, email, payment and security providers subject to a published subprocessor list and appropriate written obligations. Material additions or changes should be notified in advance under an agreed objection process. Processor remains responsible for its subprocessors as required by contract and law.

## 9. International transfers

Transfers outside Ghana or other applicable jurisdictions must follow relevant law, documented instructions and appropriate transfer safeguards. Controller should be informed of processing locations and transfer mechanisms; no blanket representation is made that all vendors remain in Ghana.

## 10. Data-subject requests

Processor shall promptly notify Controller of requests received directly about Controller data, unless prohibited, and provide reasonable technical assistance for access, correction, deletion, objections and withdrawal requests. Processor shall not independently respond on Controller’s behalf unless authorised or required by law.

## 11. Personal-data breaches

Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed personal-data breach affecting Controller data, with available details of nature, categories, likely consequences, mitigation and contact point. Updates follow as facts become known. Controller retains responsibility for applicable regulatory or individual notifications unless otherwise agreed.

## 12. Assistance, assessments and audits

Processor will provide reasonably necessary information to demonstrate compliance, support risk assessments and cooperate with proportionate audits subject to confidentiality, security and reasonable scheduling. Independent assurance reports may satisfy routine audit requests where appropriate.

## 13. Return, deletion and retention

At termination, Controller may request export or deletion of its personal data subject to an agreed transition window. Processor will delete or return data and instruct subprocessors accordingly, except where legal retention applies. Backups are purged on documented cycles and protected until deletion.

## 14. Liability and order of precedence

Liability, indemnity and governing law follow the Services Agreement unless expressly modified here; mandatory data protection obligations remain effective. If there is a conflict about processing client personal data, this DPA prevails over general service terms to the extent specified by the parties.
